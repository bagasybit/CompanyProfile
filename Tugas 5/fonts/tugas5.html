About us

Ocean Xes is a marine engineering company built for work that cannot fail: deep-water installations, subsea surveys and the daily running of large fleets.

Our engineers, divers and ship masters work to international safety and quality standards, so clients get dependable results from the first survey to the final handover.

What we do

Six core services, delivered by one team from planning to operation.

Offshore engineering

Design, installation and maintenance of platforms and marine structures.

Subsea inspection

ROV and diver-based inspection of pipelines, hulls and underwater assets.

Marine survey

Hydrographic, geophysical and vessel condition surveys with certified reporting.

Fleet management

Crewing, technical management and compliance for commercial vessels.

Port & logistics

Terminal operations, cargo handling and marine supply chain planning.

Safety & environment

HSE audits, spill response planning and coastal impact assessments.
